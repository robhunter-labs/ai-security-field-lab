# AI × Security field lab

This is where I keep the working side of my writing about AI and security: studies, benches and small harness experiments, each tied to a piece on [robhunterworks.com](https://robhunterworks.com/writing/). I'm a practitioner exploring the edges, not handing down a framework. Every entry says what was actually tested and what wasn't.

Bigger pieces live in their own repositories and are indexed here. Smaller ones get a folder on this shelf as they're written.
